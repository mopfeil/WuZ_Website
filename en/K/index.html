Please do not pass this on. These pages are not linked anywhere and are meant for you because you want to perform the tricks yourself. Keep the secrets to yourself, and practise before you show anything.

Each piece shows something about how we form a picture of the world: how easily perception and expectation fool us, and how checks, measurement and repetition help against that.
